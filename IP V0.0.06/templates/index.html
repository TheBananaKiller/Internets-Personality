Internets Personality

Welcome!

WARNING

Welcome to the Internet's Personality! This website is currently not moderated and is meant to be a representation of the internet in one canvas . Thus, you may see imagery not appropriate for all ages. You have been warned.

Info

Internet's Personality is a shared online canvas where anyone can create art, express themselves, and leave their mark on the internet. Our goal is to capture the creativity, culture, and personality of the online world within a single 16-color canvas. We hope you enjoy exploring, creating, and contributing as much as we have enjoyed building it.

There is a large update every 5 updates, going 0.0.05, 0.0.1, 0.0.15 etc.

Fun Facts!

The first edition of this website was made on a Sunday afternoon in around 6 hours.
The idea for this website comes from Spi7nix's Geometry Dash level made by his fans.
The website was originally going to have a proceduraly generated canvas depending on how far you go.
This whole website is made with love by The Banana Killer.
